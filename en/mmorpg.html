YOUR QUESTIONS

From your vision
to your first step.

Can I really build both classic and modern-style MMORPGs?

The framework aims to support different MMORPG approaches without locking you into a single ready-made game format. Use classic targeting, party, and economy systems, or design a more modern experience with free aiming, movement-driven skills, area attacks, and your own visuals. This does not mean every mechanic from the reference games is included as an exact ready-made equivalent.

Is this only for experienced developers or large teams?

The visual editor panels are being designed for solo developers and teams who want to adapt ready-made systems to their own content. As a project grows, so does the work required for content production, optimization, testing, and server operations.

Can I do everything without knowing how to code?

You can edit the supported ready-made areas without writing code. Entirely new game mechanics may require additional development. Technical knowledge is needed for preparing content in Unreal Engine, packaging, and setting up servers.

Can I create party dungeons for 5 or 8 players?

Dungeon settings support party sizes from 3 to 8 players, with configurable level requirements, tickets, and rewards. The framework includes a structure for using the same dungeon design in separate group rooms. Setup for your own map and multiplayer testing are required.

Where are the demo, pricing, and license details?

This page introduces features; it is not an announced sale or license offer. We can discuss the project’s current state, demonstration options, and a scope suited to your needs through the official contact channels.
