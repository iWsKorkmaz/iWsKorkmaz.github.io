YOUR QUESTIONS

Before
you begin.

Is this a game or a development framework?

The project presented here is a modular MMO framework and editor panels being developed in Unreal Engine. Development is ongoing. The aim is to let you shape your own MMO experience using ready-made systems.

What can I change without writing code?

You can edit the values, content, and rules supported by the ready-made panels. New mechanics may require coding. Technical knowledge is still needed for Unreal Engine, content preparation, packaging, and server management.

Can I use my own models and content?

Compatible content you have the rights to use can be linked to the relevant systems. Check skeleton and animation compatibility for characters, and the required structure and settings for other assets.

Where can I find demo, pricing, and license information?

No demo, pricing, or license offer is currently announced on this page. Follow development updates and new announcements through the official channels below.
